# 十二长生（数据来源：bazi-terms，MIT License）

长生：Birth；拼音 cháng shēng；Newly born energy; the element enters its most promising phase.
沐浴：Bathing；拼音 mù yù；Infancy and washing; vulnerable, romantic and unstable energy.
冠带：Crowning；拼音 guàn dài；Coming of age; the element dresses up and starts to show capability.
临官：Officiating；拼音 lín guān；Taking office; mature, productive energy approaching its peak.
帝旺：Peak；拼音 dì wàng；Imperial prosperity; the element at maximum strength.
衰：Decline；拼音 shuāi；Energy past its prime, gradually weakening.
病：Illness；拼音 bìng；Weakened energy, like sickness; low vitality.
死：Death；拼音 sǐ；Dormant energy; quiet, still and inactive.
墓：Tomb；拼音 mù；Stored energy locked in the tomb; latent, hoarded, awaiting a key.
绝：Extinction；拼音 jué；The lowest point; energy seemingly gone but about to turn.
胎：Gestation；拼音 tāi；Conception; a new cycle forming in secret.
养：Nurturing；拼音 yǎng；Nourishment before birth; gentle preparation for the next cycle.
