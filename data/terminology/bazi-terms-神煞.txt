# 神煞（数据来源：bazi-terms，MIT License）

天乙贵人：Heavenly Nobleman；拼音 tiān yǐ guì rén；The most auspicious star: helpful people and timely rescue in difficulty.
太极贵人：Taiji Nobleman；拼音 tài jí guì rén；Affinity with metaphysics, philosophy and deep research.
天德贵人：Heavenly Virtue；拼音 tiān dé guì rén；Heaven-sent protection that softens disasters.
月德贵人：Monthly Virtue；拼音 yuè dé guì rén；Gentle protective virtue; calming influence on misfortune.
天德合：Heavenly Virtue Union；拼音 tiān dé hé；Companion star of Heavenly Virtue; reinforces protection.
月德合：Monthly Virtue Union；拼音 yuè dé hé；Companion star of Monthly Virtue; reinforces protection.
德秀贵人：Virtue-Elegance Nobleman；拼音 dé xiù guì rén；Refined character and talent; turns danger into fortune.
福星贵人：Blessing Nobleman；拼音 fú xīng guì rén；Everyday blessings, comfort and a smooth life path.
文昌贵人：Literary Star；拼音 wén chāng guì rén；Academic success, writing talent and examination luck.
学堂：School Hall；拼音 xué táng；Scholarly star favoring study and intellectual growth.
词馆：Literary Bureau；拼音 cí guǎn；Eloquence and literary accomplishment.
天医：Heavenly Doctor；拼音 tiān yī；Affinity with medicine, healing and health work.
驿马：Traveling Horse；拼音 yì mǎ；Movement: travel, relocation, overseas ties and restless change.
桃花：Peach Blossom；拼音 táo huā；Romance, charm and social magnetism.
红艳：Red Beauty；拼音 hóng yàn；Intense romantic allure; passion that can outshine caution.
红鸾：Red Phoenix；拼音 hóng luán；Marriage and celebration star; joyous occasions.
天喜：Heavenly Joy；拼音 tiān xǐ；Happiness, weddings and festive events.
华盖：Elegant Canopy；拼音 huá gài；Artistic genius and spiritual depth, tinged with solitude.
金舆：Golden Carriage；拼音 jīn yú；Comfort, spousal support and an easy ride through life.
将星：General Star；拼音 jiàng xīng；Leadership, authority and command over others.
羊刃：Blade；拼音 yáng rèn；Sharp, aggressive edge just past the peak; courage mixed with risk of injury.
飞刃：Flying Blade；拼音 fēi rèn；The opposite-facing blade; sudden conflicts and impulsive force.
禄神：Prosperity Star；拼音 lù shén；Steady income and self-earned wealth; the stem at its own root.
流霞：Flowing Mist；拼音 liú xiá；Blood-related caution star; watch for injuries and inflammation.
孤辰：Solitary Star；拼音 gū chén；Emotional distance and preference for solitude.
寡宿：Widowed Star；拼音 guǎ sù；Loneliness in close relationships; inner detachment.
劫煞：Robbery Star；拼音 jié shà；Sudden loss or seizure; act with caution in risky matters.
灾煞：Disaster Star；拼音 zāi shà；External misfortune; traditionally linked to accidents and setbacks.
亡神：Lost Spirit；拼音 wáng shén；Hidden schemes and mental drain; can sharpen strategy when controlled.
披麻：Mourning Veil；拼音 pī má；Family worries and melancholy events.
童子煞：Child Star；拼音 tóng zǐ shà；Folk belief of a celestial child identity; a youthful, otherworldly temperament.
六秀日：Six Elegance Day；拼音 liù xiù rì；Day pillar known for talent and refined appearance.
九丑日：Nine Ugly Day；拼音 jiǔ chǒu rì；Day pillar linked to romantic entanglements; name misleads, effect is relational friction.
空亡：Void；拼音 kōng wáng；The two branches outside the current decade cycle; energy that feels hollow or deferred.
国印：State Seal；拼音 guó yìn；Officialdom, seals of office and institutional power.
天罗地网：Sky Net & Earth Net；拼音 tiān luó dì wǎng；Confining combinations (戌亥 / 辰巳) that feel like being trapped.
阴差阳错：Yin-Yang Mismatch；拼音 yīn chā yáng cuò；Day pillar linked to misunderstandings in family and marriage matters.
十恶大败：Ten Evils Great Defeat；拼音 shí è dà bài；Classic inauspicious day pillar group; tradition advises careful money management.
