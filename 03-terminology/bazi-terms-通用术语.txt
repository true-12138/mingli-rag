# 通用术语（数据来源：bazi-terms，MIT License）

八字：BaZi (Four Pillars)；拼音 bā zì；The Eight Characters: a destiny chart built from the year, month, day and hour of birth.
四柱：Four Pillars；拼音 sì zhù；The four stem-branch pairs — year, month, day and hour — that make up a chart.
年柱：Year Pillar；拼音 nián zhù；The first pillar; roots, ancestry and early life.
月柱：Month Pillar；拼音 yuè zhù；The second pillar; parents, career environment and youth.
日柱：Day Pillar；拼音 rì zhù；The third pillar; the self (stem) and the spouse palace (branch).
时柱：Hour Pillar；拼音 shí zhù；The fourth pillar; children, later life and ambitions.
天干：Heavenly Stem；拼音 tiān gān；One of ten yang/yin symbols; the visible, active layer of a pillar.
地支：Earthly Branch；拼音 dì zhī；One of twelve symbols (the zodiac animals); the hidden, receptive layer of a pillar.
干支：Stem-Branch (GanZhi)；拼音 gān zhī；A stem paired with a branch; one unit of the sexagenary cycle.
藏干：Hidden Stems；拼音 cáng gān；The stems concealed inside each branch, giving it layered meaning.
大运：Luck Pillar；拼音 dà yùn；A ten-year fortune cycle overlaid on the natal chart.
流年：Annual Fortune；拼音 liú nián；The influence of a specific calendar year on the chart.
小运：Minor Cycle；拼音 xiǎo yùn；A one-year cycle used before the first Luck Pillar begins.
起运：Luck Onset；拼音 qǐ yùn；The age at which the first Luck Pillar cycle starts.
命宫：Life Palace；拼音 mìng gōng；An auxiliary pillar describing the core life theme.
身宫：Body Palace；拼音 shēn gōng；An auxiliary pillar describing how life is lived out in practice.
胎元：Conception Pillar；拼音 tāi yuán；The stem-branch of conception; inherited and prenatal influence.
胎息：Prenatal Breath；拼音 tāi xī；A pillar paired with the Conception Pillar; subtle life force.
阴阳：Yin & Yang；拼音 yīn yáng；The two complementary polarities underlying every stem and branch.
五行：Five Elements；拼音 wǔ xíng；Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water — the phases of transforming energy.
十神：Ten Gods；拼音 shí shén；Relationship labels between each stem and the Day Master.
神煞：Shen Sha (Symbolic Stars)；拼音 shén shà；Auspicious and inauspicious symbolic stars overlaid on the chart.
纳音：Na Yin (Melodic Element)；拼音 nà yīn；The thirty melodic elements assigned to each stem-branch pair.
刑冲合会：Interactions；拼音 xíng chōng hé huì；The clashes, combinations, punishments, harms and destructions between stems and branches.
生肖：Chinese Zodiac；拼音 shēng xiào；The twelve animals mapped to the Earthly Branches.
旬：Decade (Xun)；拼音 xún；A ten-day group of the sexagenary cycle; determines the void branches.
星运：Growth Stage；拼音 xīng yùn；One of the twelve lifecycle stages of an element across the branches.
自坐：Self-sitting；拼音 zì zuò；The branch directly beneath the Day Stem and the ten god it forms.
五行分值：Element Score；拼音 wǔ xíng fēn zhí；Weighted strength of each of the Five Elements in the chart.
主星：Main Star；拼音 zhǔ xīng；The primary ten god of a pillar (usually its hidden main stem).
副星：Secondary Star；拼音 fù xīng；The additional ten gods contributed by other hidden stems.
日主五行：Day Master Element；拼音 rì zhǔ wǔ xíng；The Five Element of the Day Stem — the self element of the chart.
真太阳时：True Solar Time；拼音 zhēn tài yáng shí；Local apparent solar time, corrected for longitude and season; the basis of the Hour Pillar.
时辰：Double-hour；拼音 shí chén；A traditional two-hour period; twelve of them span a day.
农历：Lunar Calendar；拼音 nóng lì；The traditional Chinese lunisolar calendar.
公历：Gregorian Calendar；拼音 gōng lì；The international solar calendar.
性别：Gender；拼音 xìng bié；Male or female; determines the direction of the Luck Pillar cycles.
男：Male；Male gender input for chart calculation.
女：Female；Female gender input for chart calculation.
顺行：Forward；拼音 shùn xíng；Luck Pillars advancing in the natural cycle order.
逆行：Backward；拼音 nì xíng；Luck Pillars advancing in the reverse cycle order.
当前：Current；拼音 dāng qián；Marks the Luck Pillar or year that is active now.
